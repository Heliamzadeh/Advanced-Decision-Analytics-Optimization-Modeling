## Project Overview

This project develops a **formal decision analytics and optimization framework** to support rational decision-making under real-world constraints. The work applies **quantitative modeling, optimization logic, and scenario analysis** to evaluate competing alternatives and identify optimal solutions based on explicit objectives and constraints.

The project is grounded in a **realistic supply-chain decision case** involving a **milk supplier**, where strategic choices must be made under capacity, cost, and operational limitations. Rather than relying on intuition or heuristics, the analysis demonstrates how **structured optimization models** produce defensible, data-driven decisions.

---

## Application Context: Milk Supplier Decision Problem

### Business Case
The assignment models a **milk supplier operating under constrained production and distribution capacity**. The supplier must decide **how to allocate milk supply across competing options** in order to optimize overall performance.

Key challenges include:
- Limited production capacity
- Multiple decision alternatives (e.g., suppliers, contracts, or distribution channels)
- Cost and revenue trade-offs
- Operational and policy constraints

The central question is:

> **How should the milk supplier allocate resources to maximize the objective (e.g., profit, cost efficiency, or utility) while respecting real-world constraints?**

---

## Problem Formulation

The milk supplier problem is framed as a **constrained optimization problem**, consisting of:

### Decision Variables
- Quantities of milk allocated to each option or channel

### Objective Function
- Optimization of a performance metric (e.g., profit maximization or cost minimization)

### Constraints
- Production capacity limits
- Demand or contract requirements
- Feasibility and non-negativity constraints

This formulation ensures decisions are:
- Explicit
- Reproducible
- Justifiable under scrutiny
